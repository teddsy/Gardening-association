Join us

Membership is open to everyone and costs just £5 per year.

What’s included

  • Access to monthly meetings and talks
  • Access to members group seed purchase and produce, plants & seeds (members area)
  • Garden visits and social events
  • Local growing advice and community
  • Support for village gardening projects

How to join

  • Come along to a meeting, or get in touch by email
  • Membership is £5 per year
  • There’s also a small door fee at meetings (usually a couple of pounds)
