Monthly Jobs

A practical month-by-month guide for Ruddington and East Midlands gardens and allotments (clay soil & no-dig focus). This is the jobs list, not the meetings calendar.

January

  • Plan next year’s beds and crop rotation
  • Order seeds and seed potatoes
  • Prune apples, pears and soft fruit (not stone fruit)
  • Clean and oil tools
  • Check stored produce and compost bins
  • Chit first-early potatoes towards the end of the month

February

  • Chit seed potatoes in a cool light place
  • Sow early crops under cover (broad beans, peas, onions, leeks, lettuce, early brassicas)
  • Prepare beds if soil is dry enough — do not walk on wet clay
  • Apply compost mulch to empty beds

March

  • Sow hardy seeds outdoors or under cover (broad beans, peas, carrots, parsnips, spinach, radish)
  • Plant first-early potatoes late in the month
  • Start tomatoes, peppers and aubergines indoors
  • Watch for late frosts

April

  • Main outdoor sowing season
  • Plant second-early potatoes, and earth up or mulch earlier plantings
  • Plant out hardened-off hardy plants; harden tender seedlings but do not plant them out yet — keep fleece ready
  • Protect young plants from slugs and pigeons

May

  • Plant tender crops after mid-month (after frost risk)
  • Sow beans, courgettes, cucumbers under cover then plant out
  • Succession sow salads, radish and French beans
  • Net brassicas against pigeons and hoe little and often
  • Mulch beds to conserve moisture

June

  • Plant out remaining tender crops
  • Start successional sowings of beans and salads
  • Water carefully – clay holds moisture but surface can dry
  • Stake and support climbing plants
  • First harvests of broad beans and early potatoes; watch for blackfly and blight

July

  • Harvest early potatoes, peas, broad beans, salads
  • Sow autumn and winter crops (kale, spring cabbage, swede, winter salads, green manures)
  • Lift garlic, and keep a blight watch on potatoes and tomatoes
  • Keep harvesting courgettes and beans regularly
  • Water in dry spells

August

  • Main harvest period
  • Sow green manures, overwintering salads and spring cabbage on empty beds
  • Order garlic for autumn planting
  • Lift and dry onions

September

  • Plant garlic (from mid-month) and plant out spring cabbage
  • Lift maincrop potatoes as the foliage dies — do not leave them in wet clay — and cure squash
  • Clear summer crops and compost them
  • Sow winter salads under cover
  • Plant spring bulbs

October

  • Mulch beds with compost or leaf mould
  • Plant spring bulbs, garlic and overwintering onion sets if not done
  • Sow overwintering broad beans such as Aquadulce
  • Lift and store tender crops (potatoes, carrots)
  • Protect brassicas with netting

November

  • Clear remaining spent crops
  • Heavy mulch on empty beds and around rhubarb
  • Last garlic plantings only if the ground is still workable
  • Plant garlic and spring bulbs
  • Protect tender plants and move pots to shelter
  • Order seed potatoes for next year

December

  • Rest and plan the year ahead
  • Protect winter brassicas from pigeons
  • Check tools and order any replacements
  • Enjoy stored produce, and mulch parsnips
  • Order seeds for next year
