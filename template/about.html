About the club

Your Gardening Association is example text for a local gardening club. Replace this paragraph with your own story, and add your founding year if you have one.

Who can join?

The club is open to everyone in Yourtown and beyond, of all ages and abilities:

  • Armchair gardeners
  • Patio and window-box planters
  • Those more ‘green fingered’
  • Allotment holders

What we do

  • Monthly meetings with speakers and social evenings
  • Members group seed purchase and produce, plants & seeds sharing
  • Garden visits and outings (including RHS gardens)
  • Support for local allotments (North Field Allotments, Yourtown Community Beds)
  • Involvement with Yourtown Open Gardens (charity event)
  • Sharing practical local growing knowledge
