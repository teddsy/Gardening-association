Meetings & Events

Monthly Meetings

  • When: last Wednesday of most months, 7.30pm
  • Where: The Hermitage, Wilford Lane, Ruddington
  • What: talks by guest speakers, members’ evenings, quizzes and social gatherings
  • Cost: membership £5 per year, plus a small door fee at meetings

Typical programme

  • Illustrated talks on plants, history, wildlife and practical growing
  • Produce, plants & seeds sharing
  • Cheese & wine / social evenings
  • Annual AGM

Other events

  • Ruddington Open Gardens (charity weekend – usually June)
  • Garden visits and coach trips
  • Village Horticultural Show involvement
  • Produce, plants & seeds sharing at coffee mornings

For the latest programme check the village diary or contact the Association.
