How it works

  1. Browse a category or crop, and choose page view (cards) or list view (one line per product).
  2. Add seeds to your basket – it is saved in this browser only.
  3. Open the basket, check the Regular, Members and You save totals, then print the summary and give it to Mike Davey with payment as arranged at the club.

Demo – no payment is taken and nothing is sent online. Deadline for orders: 31 January 2027.
